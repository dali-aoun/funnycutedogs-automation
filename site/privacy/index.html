Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you visit funnycutedogs.com or watch our videos on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. We run a small dog-content channel, and we keep data collection to the minimum.

1. Who we are

Funny & Cute Dogs (“we”, “us”) publishes dog behavior videos and short clips on YouTube (@funnycutedogs), Instagram (@dogslover_petslover) and TikTok. This website is a simple link page that points to those channels and to a dog training resource.

2. What this website collects

The website has no accounts, no forms, no comments and no newsletter. We do not ask you for your name, email address or any other personal details, and we do not knowingly collect them.

Like any website, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes basic technical data when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type, country and the pages requested. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and understand overall traffic. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors.

3. Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies on funnycutedogs.com. Third-party sites you reach through our links may set their own cookies (see section 4).

4. Affiliate links and third-party sites

Some links on this site and in our video descriptions are affiliate links, for example the link labelled “Free Dog Training Tips”, which leads to the Brain Training for Dogs program through ClickBank. If you click one and later buy, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

When you follow such a link you leave our site. The destination site and its payment or affiliate network (for example ClickBank) collect and use data under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. Please read them before sharing personal information there.

5. Social media platforms and publishing tools

Our videos are hosted on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. When you watch, like, comment or follow, those platforms collect data according to their own policies:

  • YouTube: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Instagram: privacycenter.instagram.com/policy
  • TikTok: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy

We use an automated tool to publish our own videos to our own accounts through the official platform APIs (YouTube Data API, Instagram Graph API and TikTok Content Posting API). The tool is authorized only for our own accounts, uses access tokens that are kept as private secrets, and is used only to upload and publish our own content. It does not collect, store or share any data about viewers, followers or other TikTok, Instagram or YouTube users.

6. Children

Our content is about dogs and is suitable for a general audience, but this website and our channels are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your choices

Because we do not collect personal information through this website, there is nothing to access or delete here. To manage the data that YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or ClickBank hold about you, use the privacy settings of those services.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a direct message to @dogslover_petslover on Instagram or through the @funnycutedogs YouTube channel.
